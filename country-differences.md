# Country-Specific Application Guide

## How to use this guide

Treat this as a comparison aid, not a source of final admissions facts. When a program-specific source conflicts with this guide, the program-specific source wins.

## United States

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Review style | Holistic; GPA, GRE, research, letters, and SOP all matter. PhD decisions often combine committee review and advisor fit. |
| GPA signal | 3.0 is below the common applicant average, but strong research and writing can offset it. |
| GRE | Many programs dropped it, but some still require or consider it for the 2027 cycle. |
| Funding | PhD is usually fully funded; Master funding is less common and often partial. |
| Deadlines | PhD deadlines usually cluster around Dec 1 to Jan 15. |

## Canada

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Review style | Research-oriented; thesis-based Master programs often feed into PhD tracks. |
| GPA signal | U of T 3.0 is often within range for many Master programs. |
| GRE | Usually not required. |
| Funding | Thesis-based Master funding is often available; PhD is typically funded. |
| Deadlines | Often between Feb 1 and Apr 1, with some variation by program. |

## United Kingdom

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Review style | Degree classification matters more than raw GPA. |
| GPA signal | U of T 3.0 is roughly comparable to a 2:1 for many institutions, but top programs remain competitive. |
| GRE | Rarely required. |
| Funding | International Master funding is limited and competitive. |
| Deadlines | Often rolling, but competitive programs can close early in the fall or winter. |

## Switzerland

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Review style | Course matching is strict; prerequisites matter a lot. |
| GPA signal | U of T 3.0 can be viable, but the academic fit must be strong. |
| GRE | Not typically required for Master programs. |
| Funding | Tuition is low, but scholarships are limited. |
| Deadlines | ETH and EPFL often have early autumn intake deadlines. |

## Singapore

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Review style | Strong regional recognition of U of T; research experience is useful. |
| GPA signal | U of T 3.0 is usually within range. |
| GRE | Program-specific. |
| Funding | Master is often self-funded; PhD funding may come via scholarships. |
| Deadlines | Intake timing varies by program and scholarship cycle. |

## Hong Kong

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Review style | English-medium universities; funding and scholarship timing matter. |
| GPA signal | U of T 3.0 is usually within range. |
| GRE | Mixed; some programs waive it for strong applicants. |
| Funding | Master is typically self-funded; PhD funding is competitive. |
| Deadlines | Program deadlines and scholarship deadlines may differ. |

## Australia

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Review style | Coursework and thesis pathways both exist; program structure varies a lot. |
| GPA signal | U of T 3.0 is often acceptable for many programs. |
| GRE | Not commonly required. |
| Funding | Master is usually self-funded; PhD scholarships are competitive. |
| Deadlines | Intake cycles are commonly February or July. |
