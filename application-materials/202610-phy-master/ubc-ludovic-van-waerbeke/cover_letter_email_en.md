I am a Physics Specialist undergraduate at the University of Toronto, expecting to graduate in June 2027 and interested in UBC's Astronomy MSc for Fall 2027. Your co-authored paper, [Measuring satellite galaxy subhalo masses in redMaPPer clusters with UNIONS weak lensing data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14207), connects with my interest in recovering halo properties from observations. Its treatment of subhalo mass alongside the host-halo contribution and uncertainty in the cluster centre is particularly relevant to questions I encountered about model assumptions and parameter degeneracy.

During 2025–2026, I completed a MaNGA project on the halo concentration–mass relation independently. I learned PyMC Bayesian modelling and MCMC to characterize mass–concentration degeneracy in rotation-curve fits. I then used prior-corrected importance sampling to infer the relation's slope, normalization and intrinsic scatter from joint posterior samples for 620 selected disk galaxies, with prior-sensitivity, inclination and PSIS checks to assess limitations. I wrote a first-author manuscript, now complete but not submitted; a preview is available at https://hyi03.github.io/manga-dm/paper-preview.

I also work under Professor Mario Campanelli at UCL on simulated Higgs four-lepton events, using pyhf profile-likelihood inference, event-group bootstrap and conditional coverage diagnostics to examine finite-sample uncertainty and interval calibration. These results remain exploratory, and a first-author manuscript is in progress.

For an MSc project, I would be interested in learning weak-lensing mass modelling through a focused simulation study: generating satellite lensing profiles with host-halo miscentring and comparing subhalo mass recovery when that effect is modelled or omitted. This would extend my experience in testing inference assumptions, while requiring further training in lensing observables and systematics.

Might you be considering MSc students for Fall 2027, and could my background be relevant to research in your group? I would be happy to provide my CV and further research details. Thank you for your time.
