## 3. Application routes and deadlines

### 3.1 Faculty of Physics aptitude application

The Faculty of Physics requires an online application through its aptitude-assessment portal.

| Intended start | Faculty deadline | Degree completion deadline |
|---|---:|---:|
| Winter semester | **15 July** | 30 September |
| Summer semester | **15 January** | 31 March |

The portal normally opens approximately two to three months before the deadline. An applicant may apply to this MSc Physics aptitude procedure only twice.

For Winter Semester 2027/28, HY should plan around **15 July 2027**. This is the current recurring deadline; the 2027 portal and instructions must be checked when they open.

### 3.2 LMU International Office application

LMU states that international applicants normally apply through the International Office. A degree program with an aptitude assessment also requires a separate, timely application to the responsible institute. The central recurring deadlines are also 15 July for winter and 15 January for summer, and the deadline is based on **receipt by LMU**.

For HY, the safe working assumption is therefore:

1. Submit the Faculty of Physics aptitude application online.
2. Submit the International Office application and certified documents by the central deadline.
3. Complete later enrollment steps after receiving the faculty decision.

Citizenship, German educational history, and any `Bildungsinländer` status can change the central route. HY should confirm the route with International Admissions before preparing certified paper copies.

## 4. Academic eligibility

The published academic entry condition is a first professional university degree, or equivalent, from Germany or abroad in physics, astrophysics, or another mathematical/scientific field.

The aptitude regulations identify the expected physics preparation as:

- atomic and molecular physics;
- nuclear and particle physics;
- solid-state physics;
- quantum mechanics;
- electrodynamics;
- statistical physics;
- mathematical and analytical ability; and
- ability to conduct scientific work.

LMU does not publish a numerical GPA minimum or a mechanical GPA scoring formula for this program. Admission therefore cannot be predicted from HY's 3.0/4.0 alone. The transcript, essay, subject GRE, questionnaire, and evidence of independent scientific work must together establish readiness.

## 5. Required application documents

### 5.1 Faculty aptitude portal

The Faculty of Physics lists:

1. Tabular curriculum vitae.
2. First-degree certificate; if unavailable, a current transcript of records.
3. An essay of no more than **1,000 words**, in German or English.
4. GRE scores in **Physics or Mathematics** for applicants whose first degree was not completed at a university in an EU country.
5. The questionnaire provided in the online portal.

The essay must explain the applicant's interest and ability for the MSc Physics program and describe prior academic achievement in detail. LMU specifically suggests discussing special lectures, the bachelor's thesis or comparable work, and the areas the applicant intends to pursue in the master's program.

### 5.2 International Office file

The current central instructions require international applicants to prepare, as applicable:

- the international application form;
- university entrance qualification or school-leaving documentation;
- university degree certificates and complete transcripts;
- officially certified copies and certified translations when required;
- an updated résumé;
- the program acceptance letter, which may be supplied later for registration; and
- an APS certificate when the applicant's school-leaving qualification and/or university degree falls under LMU's PR China, India, or Vietnam rule.

The International Office states that recommendation letters, expert reports, and internship certificates are not required for its part of the process. English-language documents do not require translation.

### 5.3 APS and English evidence

- **APS:** HY's U of T degree alone does not establish whether APS is required. LMU's central rule also refers to school-leaving certificates. If HY completed secondary school in mainland China, the APS question should be raised with International Admissions now.
- **English:** The MSc Physics faculty checklist does not list TOEFL or IELTS, and the program is taught in English. An official U of T letter confirming English as the language of instruction is still the safest evidence to prepare. Whether it must be uploaded should be confirmed when the 2027 portal opens.

## 6. How the aptitude assessment works

The published regulations describe a two-stage process.

### Stage 1: written file and essay

Two members of the selection committee assess the essay. If both independently judge the applicant suitable, the applicant passes at this stage. If both judge the applicant unsuitable, the applicant is rejected. If their judgments differ, the applicant proceeds to an interview.

The regulations do not publish a numerical weight for GPA or a minimum GRE percentile. HY should not infer an unofficial cutoff.

### Stage 2: structured interview

The interview lasts about 30 minutes and covers:

- previous academic training;
- physics knowledge in the expected subject areas;
- experience with independent scientific work; and
- capacity for a scientific approach to problems.

The faculty states that results are normally communicated approximately two to three weeks after the application deadline.

## 7. Applicant-specific fit and risk analysis

### Strengths to foreground

1. **Direct physics preparation:** a University of Toronto physics degree is clearly within the intended academic field.
2. **Quantitative research identity:** the MaNGA project demonstrates Bayesian model construction, PyMC 5, MCMC diagnostics, uncertainty analysis, and astrophysical interpretation.
3. **Breadth across dark-matter scales:** the UCL project adds computational particle physics and supports a bridge from galaxy-scale inference to fundamental interactions.
4. **Research trajectory:** a first-author manuscript and publication-quality UCL work demonstrate preparation for LMU's full-year master's project.
5. **Program-specific fit:** the Astrophysics and AI in Physics specializations permit a credible curriculum rather than a generic interest in physics.

### Material risks

1. **Subject GRE is currently missing.** This is the clearest procedural blocker.
2. **The cumulative GPA is not a strength.** The application must show stronger performance in advanced, relevant courses where the transcript supports that claim.
3. **Course breadth may be audited.** Solid-state, atomic/molecular, and advanced laboratory preparation should not be assumed from the degree title.
4. **Research cannot conceal missing prerequisites.** Projects strengthen scientific-readiness evidence but do not replace missing foundational coursework.
5. **The International Office route may require certified paper documents and APS.** Late clarification would create a deadline risk.

## 9. Essay plan: maximum 1,000 words

The essay is an admissions instrument, not a general personal statement. A strong LMU version should use approximately:

| Section | Target length | Content |
|---|---:|---|
| Research question and motivation | 100–120 words | Dark matter as an inference problem linking astrophysics, computation, and particle physics |
| Academic preparation | 160–190 words | Relevant advanced physics and mathematics; emphasize stronger upper-level results factually |
| MaNGA project | 230–260 words | Scientific question, data, PyMC/MCMC method, validation, result status, and manuscript |
| UCL project | 180–220 words | Exact physical question, numerical method, personal contribution, validation, and output target |
| Why LMU | 180–210 words | Astrophysics/AI specialization, named course choices, full-year research phase, and one or two research groups |
| Future objective | 60–90 words | Computational dark-matter or particle-physics research leading toward a PhD |

The GPA should not dominate the essay. If there is a specific, documentable explanation, address it briefly and then show later evidence of readiness. Do not claim that research compensates for missing required courses or the subject GRE.
