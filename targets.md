# 申请目标

## 1. Application strategy

- Physics PhD: focus on the United States and Singapore, with self-funded Singapore options only if the advisor fit is strong
- Physics Master: Canada, USA, UK, Switzerland, Germany, France, Singapore, Hong Kong, Australia
- Data Science Master: Canada, USA, UK, Switzerland, Singapore, Hong Kong, Australia

## 5. Priority rules

- High priority means strong fit plus deadline pressure or funding importance.
- Keep a separate flag for programs that are deadline-near, even if the fit is only medium.
- Do not mark a program as safe unless the requirements and typical admitted profile are actually verified.
