# Senior-Year Course Selection

Goal: complete the Physics Specialist requirements first, while keeping the course plan aligned with graduate applications in computational physics, statistical modeling, data analysis, and ML for particle physics.

## Physics-Related Courses: 4.5 Credits

| Order | Requirement Type | Selection Method (in Recommended Order) | Credits | Application Value |
|---:|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Specialist: third-year additional `PHY 300/400-level` | Choose 1: `PHY407H1 Computational Physics`, `PHY408H1 Time Series Analysis`, `PHY478H1 Undergraduate Research Project`, `PHY371Y1 Quantum Information Processing`, `PHY483H1 Relativity Theory I` | 0.5 | Computational physics, Python, numerical methods, data analysis |
| 2 | Specialist: fourth-year core list | Choose 2: `PHY456H1 Quantum Mechanics II`, `PHY452H1 Statistical Mechanics`, `PHY450H1 Electromagnetic Theory`, `PHY460H1 Nonlinear Physics`, `PHY454H1 Continuum Mechanics` | 1.0 | Particle physics, statistical physics, field/continuum foundations |
| 3 | Specialist: `PHY405/407/408/426/478` group | Choose 1: `PHY478H1 Undergraduate Research Project`, `PHY407H1 Computational Physics`, `PHY408H1 Time Series Analysis`, `PHY426H1 Fluid Mechanics`, `PHY405H1 Electronics Lab` | 0.5 | Research experience, computational physics, experimental/data skills |
| 4 | Specialist: fourth-year `PHY 400-level` elective | Choose 2: `PHY408H1 Time Series Analysis`, `PHY483H1 Relativity Theory I`, `PHY407H1 Computational Physics`, `PHY452H1 Statistical Mechanics`, `PHY426H1 Fluid Mechanics`, `PHY460H1 Nonlinear Physics` | 1.0 | Astronomy/experimental data, cosmology, statistical physics |
| 5 | Elective / total credits: data analysis or computation | Choose 1: `PHY408H1 Time Series Analysis`, `PHY407H1 Computational Physics` | 0.5 | Data analysis, computational physics, statistical modeling |
| 6 | Elective / total credits: astronomy or physics direction | Choose 1: `PHY483H1 Relativity Theory I`, `PHY452H1 Statistical Mechanics`, `PHY460H1 Nonlinear Physics` | 0.5 | Astrophysics, cosmology, physics depth |
| 7 | Elective / total credits: strengthening elective | Choose 1: `PHY407H1 Computational Physics`, `PHY408H1 Time Series Analysis`, `PHY483H1 Relativity Theory I`, `PHY452H1 Statistical Mechanics` | 0.5 | Adjust based on target programs in computational physics or data analysis |

Total: 4.5 credits.
