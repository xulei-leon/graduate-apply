# University of Melbourne — Master of Science (Physics)

- **Country:** Australia
- **University:** University of Melbourne
- **Program:** Master of Science (Physics)
- **Department:** School of Physics, Faculty of Science
- **Track:** Physics / Computational Physics
- **GPA requirement:** Weighted Average Mark (WAM) of 65%+ (H3) or equivalent
- **GRE requirement:** Not required
- **TOEFL/IELTS:** IELTS 6.5 (no band < 6.0); waived if English-instruction
- **Prerequisites:** Major in Physics, Mathematical Physics, or related; QM at 2nd and 3rd year level; E&M and stat mech recommended
- **Cohort size:** ~30–50
- **Deadline:** Varies; apply early
- **Funding:** Limited; research training scheme available
- **Tuition:** AUD ~45k–55k/year (international)

- **Last verified:** 2026-05-06
- **Match:** Medium — 65% equivalent likely within range; research project component
