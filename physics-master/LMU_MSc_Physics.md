# Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München — MSc Physics

- **Country:** Germany
- **University:** Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München (LMU Munich)
- **Ranking:** QS 54
- **Program:** MSc Physics
- **Master type:** Academic/Research
- **Duration:** 2 years (4 semesters)
- **Research/thesis:** Yes (master's thesis required)
- **Department:** Faculty of Physics
- **Track:** Physics / Astrophysics / Computational Physics
- **GPA requirement:** No published numerical minimum; first degree in Physics/Astrophysics or another mathematical-scientific field plus aptitude assessment
- **GRE requirement:** Physics or Mathematics GRE required when the first degree was completed outside the EU; the General GRE does not satisfy the stated subject-knowledge requirement
- **TOEFL/IELTS:** Program taught in English; the faculty checklist does not publish a standardized-test minimum, so English-medium evidence and portal treatment must be confirmed
- **Prerequisites:** Atomic/molecular, nuclear/particle and solid-state physics; quantum mechanics, electrodynamics, statistical physics, mathematics/analysis, and independent scientific work
- **Cohort size:** Not published
- **Deadline:** July 15 (Winter), January 15 (Summer)
- **Funding:** No guaranteed MSc funding identified; LMU charges no general tuition, but the variable Studierendenwerk semester fee is required
- **Tuition:** No tuition under LMU's current general policy; semester contribution required
- **Source:** https://www.physik.lmu.de/en/studies/study-programs/applying-to-a-masters-program/
- **Last verified:** 2026-09-04
- **Match:** Medium — strong in astrophysics and computational physics; GRE Physics may help Q166
- **Notes:** The faculty requires a CV, transcript/degree, ≤1,000-word essay, subject GRE for a non-EU first degree, and portal questionnaire. The essay is reviewed by two assessors; disagreement leads to an approximately 30-minute interview. International applicants normally also apply to the LMU International Office. Can apply twice maximum. See [the detailed application analysis](../application-materials/lmu-physics-msc-application-analysis.md).
