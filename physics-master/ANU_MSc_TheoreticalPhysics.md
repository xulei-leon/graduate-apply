**Program profile:** 2-year program: advanced theoretical physics coursework + research thesis. Focus on theoretical and mathematical physics. Research areas: cosmology, particle theory, condensed matter theory, quantum science.

## Advanced option — Master of Science (Advanced) in Theoretical Physics (VSCTP)

| Field | Current record |
|---|---|
| Program code | VSCTP |
| Official title | Master of Science (Advanced) in Theoretical Physics |
| Duration | 2 years full-time |
| Minimum units | 96 units |
| Delivery | In person |
| Department / college | ANU College of Science and Medicine / Research School of Physics |
| Research component | 24-unit Masters Research Project, assessed by a written thesis of approximately 50 pages and final oral presentation |
| Coursework component | 72 units, including compulsory theoretical physics courses such as Quantum Field Theory, General Relativity, Applied Mathematical Methods in Physics, Quantum Field Theory II, and Nonlinear Dynamics in Physics |
| GPA requirement | Bachelor degree or international equivalent with minimum GPA 5.5/7.0 |
| Cognate requirement | At least 8 courses in Physics, Engineering, or Mathematics, also with minimum GPA 5.5/7.0; ANU uses the best 8 cognate courses if more than 8 have been completed |
| Progression hurdle | Must achieve at least 70% WAM in the first 48 units of coursework and receive approval from an identified supervisor to remain in the Advanced program and progress to the research project |
| Fallback rule | Students who do not meet the 70% WAM or supervisor-approval hurdle are transferred to the standard Master of Science in Theoretical Physics or a Graduate Diploma of Science |
| 2027 international fee | AUD 57,640 annual indicative fee |
| Semester 2, 2027 application deadline | Applications are open now and close on 15 May 2027 |
| Semester 2 acceptance deadline | 30 June 2027 if overseas; 10 July 2027 if in Australia when accepting the offer |

| Application source | https://study.anu.edu.au/apply/international-applications |
| Last verified | 2026-06-28 |

**Advanced option fit note:** VSCTP is a stronger research-oriented version of the ANU theoretical physics master, but it should be treated as a Reach for HY because the published GPA threshold is 5.5/7.0 for both the overall Bachelor degree and the best 8 cognate courses. It is most useful as a first preference only if ANU allows multiple preferences with the standard MSc Theoretical Physics (NSCTP) listed as a lower-risk backup.
