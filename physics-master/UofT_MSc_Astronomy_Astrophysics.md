# University of Toronto — MSc Astronomy and Astrophysics

## Program facts

| Field | Verified information |
|---|---|
| Country | Canada |
| University | University of Toronto |
| Program name | Astronomy and Astrophysics MSc |
| Graduate unit | David A. Dunlap Department of Astronomy and Astrophysics; separate from the Department of Physics |
| Track | Observational and theoretical astronomy, galaxies, cosmology, general relativity, instrumentation, and large surveys |
| Master type | Academic / research; the SGS program overview says MSc admission occurs only in some cases as a preliminary phase before proceeding to the PhD |
| Duration | 3 sessions full-time; time limit 3 years |
| Research / thesis | Two full-course-equivalent research courses, AST1501Y and AST1500Y, with different supervisors and oral examinations; at least 1.5 additional FCEs of graduate coursework |
| GPA requirement | Department: appropriate bachelor's degree with “high academic standing.” No department-specific numerical MSc cutoff is published. SGS general minimum: at least mid-B, normally in the final year or senior courses |
| GRE | SGS program overview lists standardized tests as N/A for MSc and PhD |
| TOEFL / IELTS | Applicants whose primary language is not English must demonstrate proficiency only if they graduated from a university where instruction and examination were not in English; a University of Toronto degree should satisfy this condition |
| Prerequisites | Physics and mathematics are explicitly accepted preparation because many universities do not offer extensive undergraduate astronomy training |
| Cohort size | Unresolved |
| Fall 2027 deadline | No separate MSc deadline is published on the SGS overview; confirm whether external MSc applications will be accepted before preparing the application |
| Funding | Unresolved for MSc on the checked public pages |
| Match | **High** — direct overlap with dark matter, galaxies, cosmology, survey data, Bayesian inference, and scientific computing |
| Difficulty | **High Reach** — research fit is excellent, but “high academic standing,” limited/conditional MSc intake, and the lack of a separately published MSc application route create substantial risk |
| Status | Contact `grad.sec@astro.utoronto.ca` to confirm Fall 2027 MSc intake and application procedure |
| Last verified | 2026-09-10 |

## Fit note

This is a much closer topical match than the Physics MSc for HY's MaNGA dark-matter halo inference project. It must nevertheless be treated as a separate, high-risk application rather than a second track inside Physics.
