# National University of Singapore — MSc Physics by Coursework

- **Country:** Singapore
- **University:** National University of Singapore
- **Ranking:** QS 8 / Physics 14 (2026)
- **Program:** Master of Science in Physics by Coursework
- **Master type:** Professional (taught)
- **Duration:** Track 1: 1-2 years full-time; Track 2: 2-3 years full-time
- **Research/thesis:** Optional research project equivalent to PC5286
- **Department:** Department of Physics, Faculty of Science
- **Track:** Physics / Applied Physics / related areas
- **GPA requirement:** Track 1 prefers Honours or 4-year degree; Track 2 accepts 3-year physics/applied physics or related degrees
- **GRE requirement:** Not mentioned
- **TOEFL/IELTS:** TOEFL 85 / IELTS 6.0 for applicants not exempted by English-medium instruction
- **Prerequisites:** Physics, Applied Physics, semiconductors, electronics, photonics, materials science, or related backgrounds are considered
- **Cohort size:** Not stated
- **Deadline:** Early cycle 16 May-15 July; regular cycle 1 October-15 March
- **Funding:** Self-supported; no automatic funding package stated on the public page
- **Tuition:** Not stated on the page; candidates must declare self-support and upload financial proof
- **Source:** https://www.physics.nus.edu.sg/student/master-of-science-by-coursework/
- **Additional source:** https://www.physics.nus.edu.sg/student/master-of-science-by-coursework-in-physics-cohorts-ay2022-23-and-after/
- **Last verified:** 2026-06-11
- **Match:** Match — strong quantitative physics option at a top-10 global school, but the program is course-heavy and self-funded
- **Notes:** Track 1 is the cleaner fit for a physics bachelor's. Workbook tag: professional agency recommended.

---

**Program profile:** NUS MSc Physics by Coursework has two tracks. Track 1 is 40 units and suited to physics/applied physics honors or 4-year degrees; Track 2 is 80 units and suits 3-year physics/applied physics or related backgrounds. Students may complete PC5286 in lieu of courses for a research project component.
