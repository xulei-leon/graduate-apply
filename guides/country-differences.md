# Country-Specific Application Guide

## USA

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **System** | Holistic review — GPA, GRE, research, LoRs, SOP all matter. Committee + advisor-match model for PhD |
| **GPA** | PhD programs typically expect 3.3+; 3.0 is below average but can be offset by research + publication |
| **GRE** | Many programs have dropped GRE; others still require it. Check per-program for 2027 cycle |
| **Funding** | PhD: fully funded (tuition + stipend via TA/RA). Master: mostly self-funded, some TA/RA available |
| **Tuition (Master)** | $30k–$60k/year for international |
| **Visa** | F-1 visa, OPT available post-graduation (STEM: 36 months) |
| **Key dates** | PhD: Dec 1–Jan 15 (fellowship priority). Master: Jan 15–Mar 1 |
| **Rankings** | US News & World Report for Physics / Data Science |

## Canada

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **System** | Thesis-based Master often leads to PhD. Research-oriented, advisor-matching important |
| **GPA** | U of T grad: GPA 3.0 meets minimum for many programs; Canadian schools know U of T rigour |
| **GRE** | Most Canadian programs do NOT require GRE |
| **Funding** | Master: guaranteed funding common (TA + RA, ~$18k–$25k CAD/year). PhD: fully funded |
| **Tuition** | ~$8k–$15k CAD/year domestic; ~$15k–$30k CAD/year international |
| **Visa** | PGWP (Post-Graduation Work Permit) available |
| **Key dates** | Rolling or Feb 1–Apr 1. Some have Sep 1 for January intake |
| **Note** | MSc Physics often = 2 years with thesis; MSc Data Science = 1–2 years, more structured |

## UK

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **System** | Degree classification–driven. U of T 3.0 ≈ UK Upper Second Class (2:1, 60–65%). Shorter programs (1 year Master) |
| **GPA** | Most good programs require First or 2:1. 3.0 is borderline 2:1; G5 (Oxford, Cambridge, Imperial, LSE, UCL) competitive |
| **GRE** | Rarely required. Some competitive programs (e.g. Oxford) may ask for it |
| **Funding** | Very limited for international Master's. Chevening, Gates Cambridge, Clarendon (Oxford) available but competitive |
| **Tuition** | £25k–£40k/year for international |
| **Visa** | Graduate Route visa (2 years work after graduation) |
| **Key dates** | Rolling admissions (apply early, Oct–Jan for Sep start). Oxford/Cambridge: early deadlines (Sep–Jan) |
| **Note** | "MSc" = 1 year taught Master; "MRes" = 1 year research Master |

## Switzerland

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **System** | ETH Zurich / EPFL top choices. Strong STEM reputation, low tuition, competitive |
| **GPA** | ETH/EPFL look for strong academic record. U of T 3.0 is at the lower end but U of T brand helps |
| **GRE** | Not typically required for Master |
| **Funding** | Very low tuition (CHF 730–1,500/semester even for international). Scholarships limited (ESKAS, ETH Excellence) |
| **Tuition** | CHF 730–1,500/semester |
| **Visa** | Student visa, part-time work allowed |
| **Key dates** | ETH: Dec 15 (Autumn). EPFL: Apr 15 (Autumn). Some programs open earlier |
| **Note** | Strong focus on course matching. Check prerequisite lists carefully |

## Singapore

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **System** | NUS / NTU. Strong research reputation in Asia. Master by coursework or research. |
| **GPA** | U of T 3.0 within range. Research experience is a plus |
| **GRE** | Some programs require GRE; check per program |
| **Funding** | Master: self-funded (~SGD 40k–50k total). PhD: scholarships available (NUS Research Scholarship, SINGA). Self-funded PhD possible if applicant can cover own costs |
| **Tuition** | SGD 40k–50k (Master total). PhD: SGD 20k/year with scholarship |
| **Visa** | Student pass, work pass after graduation |
| **Key dates** | Aug intake: apply by Jan–Mar. Jan intake: apply by Aug–Sep |
| **Note** | Strong U of T recognition in Singapore |

## Hong Kong

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **System** | HKU, CUHK, HKUST. English-medium universities. Master 1 year (taught) or 2 years (research) |
| **GPA** | U of T 3.0 within range; U of T brand well recognized |
| **GRE** | Some programs require; many waive for U of T grads |
| **Funding** | Master: self-funded (~HKD 150k–250k total). PhD: HKPFS (competitive) + university scholarships |
| **Tuition** | HKD 150k–250k (Master total) |
| **Visa** | Immigration scheme for graduates |
| **Key dates** | Sep intake: apply Nov–Feb. Rolling for some programs |
| **Note** | Strong U of T recognition. Check if HKPFS deadline is earlier than program deadline |

## Australia

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **System** | Australian 8 (Go8) universities. Master 1–2 years. Thesis-based or coursework |
| **GPA** | U of T 3.0 ≈ Australian WAM 65%+. Meets entry for most programs. Some competitive programs may need higher |
| **GRE** | Not commonly required |
| **Funding** | Master: self-funded. PhD: RTP (Research Training Program) scholarships available but competitive |
| **Tuition** | AUD 40k–55k/year for international |
| **Visa** | Post-study work visa (2–4 years) available |
| **Key dates** | Feb intake: apply Aug–Nov. Jul intake: apply Feb–Apr. Some schools have rolling admissions |
| **Note** | "Master of Science" 1.5–2 years; some offer research pathway to PhD |
